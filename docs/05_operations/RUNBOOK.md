# Runbook — Penatika

> **Status: Conditional.** Operational procedures cannot be finalized before deployment, observability, ownership, and support decisions.

## Activation Trigger

Activate before a persistent shared environment or classroom pilot is operated.

## Required Future Procedures

- Classroom session cannot start, pair, synchronize, reconnect, end, or save.
- Teacher-private information appears on a classroom display.
- AI or speech provider is unavailable, slow, or returning invalid output.
- Mathematics validator or curriculum source is unavailable or inconsistent.
- Raw audio, secrets, or private content appears in logs or telemetry.
- Database migration or deployment fails.
- Realtime connection volume or provider cost exceeds safe bounds.
- Security incident, unauthorized session access, or pairing abuse is detected.
- Rollback, data recovery, key rotation, and provider disablement.

## Minimum Incident Information

Future runbooks should identify owner, severity, user impact, safe containment, relevant dashboards, exact commands, rollback criteria, communication path, and evidence retention without exposing sensitive data.

## Open Decisions

- Operational owner and on-call/support model.
- Deployment platform and observability tooling.
- Severity levels and response objectives.
- Backup and recovery architecture.
- Provider escalation paths.
- Classroom pilot support process.
